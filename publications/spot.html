SPOT

Target-Constrained Prompt Selection with Image Verification for Training-Free Text-to-Image Safety

Original paper / DOI ↗

Abstract

Research summary

Text-to-image moderation must reduce harmful generations while preserving benign behavior and the safe intent of risky prompts. SPOT separates the selection of a safe rewrite from candidate generation, making the safety–semantic fidelity trade-off independently testable.

Proposed method

A frozen LLM proposes and scores rewrites; a target-constrained selector chooses the feasible candidate closest to the original. A separate vision-language model verifies the generated image before release. The method requires no safety-model training.

Experimental results

+3.3 ppRetention vs. soft selection on CoProV2
+0.022MPNet similarity vs. the same soft baseline
0%Text-target violations among feasible selections

Results from the November 2026 manuscript evaluation; accepted title and camera-ready results should be checked before publication.

Research impact

The approach makes prompt selection auditable and provides a controllable safety constraint without retraining the image generator. The text-side guarantee is conditional on a feasible candidate bank; it does not guarantee that every generated image is safe.
