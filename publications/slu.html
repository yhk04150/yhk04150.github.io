Improved Spoken Language Representation for Intent Understanding in a Task-Oriented Dialogue System

Original paper / DOI ↗

Abstract

Research summary

Speech recognition errors caused by articulation and noise can lead to incorrect intent classification. This research improves spoken intent modeling by learning from both clean labeled sentences and recognized transcriptions.

Proposed method

Jointly fine-tunes the language representation using labeled text and ASR-generated text; the recognized language model is used at inference.
