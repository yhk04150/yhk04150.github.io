ICCV Workshops · 2023 · Medical Computer Vision

ML-GCN

Enhancing Multi-Label Long-Tailed Classification on Chest X-Rays through ML-GCN Augmentation

Original paper / DOI ↗

Abstract

Research summary

Chest X-ray classification must account for multiple co-occurring labels and substantial class imbalance. This work augments a graph-based multi-label classifier to better handle both common and rare conditions.

Proposed method

Combines multiple expert models, class-balanced sampling, RIDE regularization, log-sum-exp pooling and multi-head attention.

Experimental results

0.178 → 0.276Development mAP, vanilla ML-GCN vs. final model
0.279Final test mAP

Results: Tables 2 and 3 of the published paper.

Research impact

The development and test results indicate improved benchmark classification and support further investigation of imbalanced multi-label medical imaging. They do not establish clinical diagnostic effectiveness.
