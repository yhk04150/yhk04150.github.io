NoDE

Noise-Decorrelation Embedding for Robust Long-Term Multivariate Time Series Forecasting

Original paper / DOI ↗

Abstract

Research summary

Longer input histories do not always improve multivariate forecasting: temporally correlated perturbations can amplify error as a model embeds an entire window. NoDE addresses this issue through structured, model-agnostic embeddings.

Proposed method

Downsampling, shuffle/unshuffle and shared affine mappings reduce effective lag covariance. A two-stage ACF-energy and quick-training selection protocol chooses the resolution, with a no-op option when decorrelation is not beneficial.

Research impact
