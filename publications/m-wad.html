M-WAD

Self-Supervised Multimodal Undergain Anomaly Detection for Wafer Probing

Original paper / DOI ↗Industry project →

Abstract

Research summary

Undergain faults can closely resemble normal motion signals and reduce test throughput. M-WAD detects these subtle faults by complementing temporal features with visual and textual representations.

Proposed method

Combines a self-supervised time-series backbone, residual-based images and anomaly-specific prompts through visual/text encoders and multimodal fusion.

Experimental results

0.850Average F1 across seven recipes
0.59 → 0.85F1 vs. SwinProbeFormer on undergain

F1 values refer to the undergain evaluation, not the overgain benchmark.

Research impact

Detecting undergain supports earlier identification of equipment behavior associated with accumulated process delays. The results demonstrate the value of domain-specific multimodal representations.
