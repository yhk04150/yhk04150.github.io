IEEE Access · 2021 · Speech Recognition / Voice Conversion

Voice Translation

Linguistic-Coupled Age-to-Age Voice Translation to Improve Speech Recognition Performance in Real Environments

Original paper / DOI ↗

Abstract

Research summary

Speech recognition systems trained predominantly on adult speakers may be less accurate for older speakers. This work investigates a feature adaptation layer that can improve recognition without modifying the underlying ASR model.

Proposed method

A neural voice conversion framework uses unsupervised phonology clustering and spectral feature adaptation to transform speech representations for the existing acoustic model.

Experimental results

ImprovedElderly speech recognition accuracy in reported experiments
ASR-agnosticAdaptation before the speech recognizer

The accessible publication abstract reports qualitative improvement; no numerical accuracy is asserted here.

Research impact

The method supports more inclusive voice interfaces by adapting input speech rather than retraining a commercial or open recognizer.
